## Why

Two of the four archived changes so far (`clean-fossils`, `reboot-genesis`)
triggered a false-positive warning at archive time: **"design.md reported
'ready' — not 'done' or 'skipped'"**. Each time the omission was deliberate,
grounded in the propose skill's own guidance (design is conditional, create
only if cross-cutting / new dep / complexity / ambiguity). The archive skill
doesn't share that context and warns anyway.

Future `skip_specs: true` changes — small docs-and-plumbing changes like the
provisioning-script TODO or any small drift-fix — will keep tripping this.
Cost per occurrence is ~5 seconds to confirm "Proceed". Cumulative cost over
project lifetime is small but non-zero; the mental cost of "wait, is this the
false positive or a real bug?" is arguably larger.

The right fix is upstream in OpenSpec (e.g. a `skip_design: true` marker in
`.openspec.yaml`, or reconciling propose vs archive guidance so they don't
disagree). Upstream work is out of scope for this project. This change
documents the false positive locally so future-me and future collaborators
know what to do when they see it, without pursuing an upstream fix now.

**Meta-note**: this change is itself `skip_specs: true` and will (deliberately)
omit `design.md`. It will therefore *itself* trigger the warning at archive
time. That's expected and part of what we're documenting.

## What Changes

- Add a new section **"Working with OpenSpec"** to `HACKING.md` that
  documents:
  - The design.md-warning false positive on `skip_specs: true` changes.
  - The workaround: at archive time, confirm "Proceed" when the warning
    fires; the omission is deliberate per the propose skill's conditional
    design guidance.
  - Space to accumulate additional OpenSpec quirks / conventions as we
    encounter them (so the section becomes the natural home for this kind
    of institutional knowledge).
- Add a CHANGELOG.md entry under `[Unreleased]`.

Explicitly **not** part of this change:
- Any change to the archive workflow itself, or to
  `.opencode/skills/openspec-archive-change/SKILL.md`. Those are
  regenerated by the OpenSpec CLI and edits would drift.
- Filing an upstream OpenSpec feature request. Considered and deferred.
- Documenting other known issues unrelated to OpenSpec. If we want a
  general "known issues" doc, that's a separate change.

## Capabilities

No spec-level behavior change. This change adds documentation about the
project's OpenSpec workflow. `.openspec.yaml` declares `skip_specs: true`.

## Impact

- **Code**: none.
- **Docs**: `HACKING.md` gains a "Working with OpenSpec" section.
  `CHANGELOG.md` gains one entry.
- **Workflow**: no behavior change. Every archive of a `skip_specs: true`
  change still warns; contributors now know to confirm "Proceed" without
  hesitation.
- **Future work explicitly unblocked but out of scope**:
  - Filing an upstream OpenSpec issue for `skip_design: true` or reconciling
    propose vs archive.
  - Additional OpenSpec quirks joining the same HACKING.md section as we
    discover them.
